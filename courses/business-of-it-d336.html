Course Overview

Business of IT - Applications introduces service management concepts commonly associated with ITIL. The course focuses on how IT organizations manage incidents, service requests, changes, problems, documentation, continual improvement, and value delivery.

Personal Learning Goal

My goal for this course is to understand how ITIL and service management concepts apply to real technical work, including cloud operations, network support, system administration, troubleshooting, and change control.

Applied Previous Coursework

Introduction to IT helped me understand the technical environment that IT services support, including hardware, software, networks, operating systems, databases, cloud systems, security, and troubleshooting.

Business of IT builds on that foundation by showing how technical work can be organized into repeatable services, documented processes, support workflows, and continual improvement.
